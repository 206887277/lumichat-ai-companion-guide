## Test all five products with the same scene

Do not judge these products from the landing page alone. Use one safe fictional scene everywhere:

> We meet every Thursday at a quiet rooftop greenhouse. You remember that I dislike being rushed, that the basil plant is named Harbor, and that I am trying to write a short letter I never send. Tonight I am tired but want to talk for ten minutes. Keep the tone warm, specific, and unhurried.

Then check:

1. Continue for at least 15–20 turns.
2. Correct one fact, such as renaming the plant from Harbor to Marlowe.
3. Skip a day, return, and ask what the companion remembers.
4. Change the emotional tone from tired to lightly playful.
5. Ask the companion to suggest the next small story beat without taking over the conversation.
6. Record setup time, repetition, correction quality, memory feel, media usefulness, privacy controls, and the real paid limit you hit.

The best product is the one that stays coherent with the least repeated setup while matching the kind of relationship you actually want.

## Pricing and commitment checks

Before paying for any AI companion product, verify:

- whether the free tier is only a short trial or a usable ongoing plan;
- whether memory, media, voice, or longer context are separated into higher tiers;
- whether renewal is monthly, yearly, or token-based;
- whether deleting the account also deletes chat history and generated media;
- whether the product is available on your device and in your region;
- whether the content policy matches the kind of companionship or roleplay you want.

A cheaper plan is not better if the feature you care about, such as memory continuity or media, is locked behind a different tier.

## Privacy and safety checklist

- Do not enter passwords, precise addresses, financial information, medical details, or confidential work data.
- Treat AI companions as fictional entertainment, not humans, therapists, crisis services, or professional advisers.
- Check the current minimum-age rules and content policy for every service.
- Review whether chats are used to improve models, and whether you can export or delete history.
- For romantic or adult roleplay, use fictional adults, keep clear boundaries, and avoid illegal or exploitative scenarios.
- If a conversation touches distress or mental health, step away from the chatbot and use real-world support resources when needed.
